swap_horiz Transform direction

Token transformation re-encrypts tokens between sites to enable cross-organization data linkage. Choose your role in this data exchange.

Send to partner

You are the data source. Convert your site-specific tokens to transit tokens so a partner can re-encrypt them into their site. Uses a transit key.

your_site tokens → transit tokens → partner
Receive from partner

You are the data recipient. Convert inbound transit tokens into your site-specific tokens so they are linkable in your environment. Uses a connect key.

transit tokens → your_site tokens → linkable
